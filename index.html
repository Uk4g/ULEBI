Questions, answered plainly

Ulebi watches every room at once, so nothing waits for the nurse to walk there. Turns and tasks sit on one list, in order, with nothing lost between shifts. The night writes itself down, so the morning report is ready and complete — no rewriting by hand.

The patient speaks, the words arrive as text, marked by urgency. Movement and voice become events: pain and safety come first, routine requests later, and routine questions are answered without a walk. The nurse always makes the final call. In the dark, infrared frames become line drawings — shapes and movement, never video — so movement is seen the moment it happens, and the nurse is alerted before the next round would have found it.

The dashboard and how it works

One screen for the whole ward. Every room, its current state, and one ordered list of what needs attention — most urgent first. No walking room to room to find out. The nurse opens one list, acknowledges a task, marks a turn done, or answers a patient by voice — from the same screen. Nothing is tracked on paper.
